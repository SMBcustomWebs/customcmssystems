display-1..3 and fs-1..3 are the SAME three sizes in theme.css, so the last two entries duplicate the first two. They are kept, and labeled as duplicates, rather than removed: a hero already saved as fs-1 would have a stored value missing from this list, the dropdown would show nothing selected, and the next save would silently resize that hero. Role names, not pixels - the reasoning is in text-size.htm's header. Default= | Massive=display-1 | X-Large=display-2 | Large=display-3 | Massive (same as above)=fs-1 | X-Large (same as above)=fs-2
