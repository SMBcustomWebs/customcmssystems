=========================================================================== snippets/legal/legal_fields.htm The editable schema shared by every legal page. Embedded INSIDE each template's cms:template block, which is what that block is for - declaring fields. Only logic must stay outside it. Field names repeat across the three templates on purpose. Couch scopes fields to their template, so privacy.php's lgl_body and terms.php's lgl_body are separate rows that cannot collide. One snippet, three independent sets. EFFECTIVE DATE IS TYPED, NOT TAKEN FROM THE FILE. It is tempting to print the page's modification date automatically, and it would be wrong. The date on a policy is a legal statement about when those terms took effect, not a record of when somebody last fixed a typo. Correcting a comma should not silently re-date the document people agreed to. =========================================================================== A rich text field rather than a PageBuilder block. Legal copy is a single flowing document that a lawyer hands over as prose - it wants headings, lists and links, not a layout system. Anything that needs designing is not a policy page.
