The heading, the two lines and the rule are identical on the basic and the email-only tile - only the form below them differs. They were duplicated, and the first thing that happened was that one copy got an edit button the other did not. ONE BUTTON FOR THE WHOLE HEADER. It opens the switch that removes the header, the heading and its formatting, the line count, both lines and their formatting, and the dividing rule - the entire Section Header group in one window. There were four buttons here, one per piece, and four stacked down the right of a header that is often two lines of text was more furniture than the thing it was editing. IT SITS AT THE UPPER LEFT OF THE TEXT BOX, which is a structural position rather than a per-element one. The per-element buttons this replaced sat on the right, beside the words they changed - that is the rule for editing one thing on screen. This edits the shape of the section, so it goes where the other structural controls go: top-left, just inside the content column and therefore immediately to the right of the section cluster hanging off the section's own corner. Reading left to right, section then header, the two clusters say what they own. INSIDE THE COLUMN, NOT HANGING OFF IT. The section cluster already occupies the space outside the left edge; a second stack out there would land on top of it. pt-5 WHILE EDITING IS WHAT MAKES ROOM. The button is absolutely positioned against this wrapper, and top-0 resolves to the inner edge of the border - padding does not push it down. So the 3rem of top padding becomes an empty band the button sits in, and the heading starts below it rather than underneath it. It also gives the wrapper height when the header is switched off and there is no content at all, which is what keeps the button reachable in the one state where nothing else renders. DIMMED WHENEVER THERE IS NO HEADING ON THE PAGE - whether that is because Remove Section Header is checked or because nobody has written one yet. cf_show_hdg covers both: the router only sets it inside the branch that cf_show_hdr guards, so a removed header can never report a visible heading. One test, both meanings. =========================================================================
THE WHOLE GROUP, ROWS AND ALL. THE ROW NAMES ARE IN THE LIST ON PURPOSE, which is the opposite of every other popup in this block. Those name only data fields, because functions.php:258 and bootstrap-grid.php:76 hard-delete an unprocessed container and a popup that names one can permanently break. A one-row window can afford that rule; this one cannot. addons/bootstrap-grid/theme/form_row_row.html renders a listed row as its own .row wrapper and pulls its children in with childof, so the rows are what give thirty-odd controls their column layout and their order. Leave them out and the fields flatten into one pseudo-group sorted by k_order, where every row's order-2 field lands next to every other row's order-2 field. NO MESSAGE FIELDS EVEN SO - not cnt_txt1_msg, not cnt_txt2_msg, not cnt_hr_msg. A message is the one type that has already broken this block, and it carries no setting. The field labels do that job instead: every control in here names the line it belongs to, so "Line 1 Color" and "Line 2 Color" tell the two rows apart without a heading between them. EVERY CONTROLLER IS VISIBLE IN THIS WINDOW, so nothing needs hide_fields. cnt_hdr_rmv_opt gates the group, cnt_txt_opt gates the two line rows, cnt_hdg_hr gates the three rule-style fields, and all three are in the list. Checking Remove folds the group shut in the window while leaving the checkbox above it, which is what lets it be unchecked again - and the reason that checkbox can live here at all is that this button is drawn whether or not the header renders.
