Mailing List

This form saves every address sent through it to Subscribers in the admin menu, with the date, the form it came from, the notice below, and a private unsubscribe link.

Addresses arrive as "pending" and are not sent to. The visitor gets one email asking them to confirm, and opening the link in it is what turns the record into a real subscriber. That is what keeps bots and mistyped addresses off the list: neither can open the mail, so neither ever becomes live. Nothing to configure – it happens on its own.

The notice below is what tells the visitor. It sits above the button and a copy is filed with every record, so it can be reworded but not removed.

Use the other contact tile for a plain get-in-touch form – it stores nothing. Use this one where the heading tells people they are signing up.

 THE NOTICE IS A FIELD, NOT HARDCODED IN THE THEME, for the same reason nws_purpose is a variable in foot_2col_form-scal.html: it is displayed and stored from ONE place, so the sentence on file can never drift from the sentence that was on the page. IT PRINTS WHERE THE PRIVACY LINE ALREADY PRINTS - above the button, in the same small type as every other point-of-collection line on the site. Stated once, where a person looks for it, and not dressed up as a banner. Emptying it does not silence it. The router substitutes the working sentence the moment it reads a blank, exactly as cf_subj does for the mail subject. Existing records keep the notice they were signed up under. That is correct - they were shown the old wording, not this one. Sending this form adds your email address to our mailing list once you confirm it by email. You can unsubscribe from any message we send.
