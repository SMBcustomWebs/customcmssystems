An atom that owns its whole conditional chain cannot drift. INDEPENDENT OF THE IMAGE. Color, image and overlay are now three separate always-available layers, exactly as hero does it. =========================================================================

The bottom layer. The background image sits on top of this, so lowering the image's transparency lets this color bleed through.

 Controller above the func, func above the slave, all three at root - the same level the group below sits at. showhide

Custom Background Color

A solid hex/rgba value, or a two-color gradient.

 Func at group level because all three of its slaves are children of this group. Returns show/hide - the house tokens.
