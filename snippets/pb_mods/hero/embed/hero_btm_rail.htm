 The solid colour list, not the grey shades. The swiper's own rail has always used dropdowns/solid-colors.htm; this one was left on shades_gray.htm, so the same control offered greys on a single hero and full colour on a slide. Two rails, one job, two different palettes. The full site-wide size list, not a hardcoded pair. This used to offer 'Small=8 | X-Small=9' only, so the biggest the rail could go was fs-8 - and the two labels were misleading as well, because the fs scale runs BACKWARDS: fs-1 is the largest and fs-10 the smallest, with fs-9 the body default. "Small=8" was actually a step UP from the body text, which is why the largest option still looked small. dropdowns/text-size.htm is the same list every other text control on the site uses, so the rail now reaches fs-1 and the labels mean what they say. A very large size in a thin rail will overflow it - set the Height of Rail to match rather than expecting the rail to grow.
