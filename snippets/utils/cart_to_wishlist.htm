============================================================ CART -> WISHLIST ------------------------------------------------------------ Saves every line currently in the cart onto the signed-in user's wishlist. Used by users/logout.php so a full cart is not simply thrown away at sign-out. CONTRACT in : none - reads the live cart and ccs_auth_uid out : c2w_saved number of lines written c2w_dupe number already on the wishlist c2w_skipped number that could not be rebuilt TEMPLATE-AGNOSTIC BY CONSTRUCTION Nothing here names product.php or service.php. A cart line records a page id; utils/item_tpl.htm reads the template that page belongs to straight out of the database. So anything sellable can be saved - services, shirts, shoes, whatever is added next - with no list here to keep in step. The requirement is only what Couch already imposes on a sellable template: a pp_price field, and pp_options when it has variants (cart.php:267 and :310 - pp_options is optional, pp_price is not). VARIANTS Primary source is raw_variants, the custom field the cart_alter_custom_fields listener in cart_ex.php:566 attaches to every line at add-to-cart time.
