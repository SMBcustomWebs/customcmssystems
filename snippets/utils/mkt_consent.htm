This exact string is SHOWN beside the box and STORED on the subscriber record as subscriber_consent_txt. Two pages offer the same tick, so two copies of the sentence would be two things to keep in step - and the one that fell behind would be the one filed against somebody's name. It is deliberately NOT a field in globals.php. An author rewording it in the admin would change what NEW records say while every existing record kept the old sentence, which is correct, but they would have no way to see that had happened. A change here is a deployment, which is visible. foot_2col_form-scal.html holds nws_purpose the same way for the newsletter box, and cnt_sub.htm holds the email-only tile's notice as a field because that one is genuinely per-tile. Three forms, three sentences, each defined exactly once. IT DESCRIBES MARKETING AND NOTHING ELSE. Order confirmations, password resets and account mail are not covered by this and must never be gated on it - they are the service the person asked for, and unticking this box does not stop them. ===========================================================================
