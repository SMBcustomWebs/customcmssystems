=========================================================================== snippets/utils/privacy_notice.htm The line that sits next to a submit control telling someone what happens to what they are about to type, with a link to the Privacy Policy. WHY IT EXISTS AT ALL. A privacy policy linked only from the footer is not where anyone reads it. The link that matters is the one at the point of collection - beside the button, at the moment the person is deciding whether to hand something over. That is also the specific thing regulators look for, and until now this site had it nowhere: not on the contact forms, not on registration, not on the newsletter sign-up. ONE FILE, FIVE CALLERS, FOR THE SAME REASON AS legal_links.htm. The wording of a privacy line is exactly the kind of thing that gets improved in one place and forgotten in four others. HOW TO USE IT cms:set pn_purpose='We use your email only to send the newsletter.' cms:embed 'utils/privacy_notice.htm' pn_purpose is optional. Omit it and a generic sentence is used. Set it and say something true and specific about THIS form - a purpose statement that could describe any form on any site is not really a purpose statement. IT CLEARS pn_purpose ON THE WAY OUT. Several blocks can render on one page, and a value left set by the first would silently caption the second - the same bleed described in CLAUDE.md sec.4b. Consuming it here means a caller that forgets to set it gets the generic line rather than the previous block's line. SAFE INSIDE A CACHED PAGEBUILDER TILE. The contact block is a tile, so its HTML is cached in one entry shared by every visitor. Everything here is visitor-INDEPENDENT: a globals field and static text. Do not add a cms:if on a consent variable or on k_logged_in. A MISSING PRIVACY PAGE DEGRADES, IT DOES NOT BREAK. If ccs_gl_privacy_link is empty the sentence still prints and the link is simply left off, rather than emitting a dead href. The field carries a cms:link default in globals.php, so it self-populates once the template is registered. ===========================================================================
